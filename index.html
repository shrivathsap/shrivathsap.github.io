Picture of the month

This is a young grey langur. The Semnopithecus is a genus native to the Indian subcontinent. These monkeys spend half their time on the ground and the other half on trees. They are primarily herbivores with non-plant food being some insects. They have a very rich social structure which cannot be described in such a short space! Interesting monkeys.
