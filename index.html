Picture of the month

"God has an inordinate fondness for beetles" is a quote attributed to J.B.S. Haldane. There are so many species of beetles that 25% of all known animal species are beetles! Pictured here is a male European rhinoceros beetle. There are over 1500 species of rhinoceros beetles, found pretty much across the world. They are large, harmless, with a thick exoskeleton and they can fly (though not very well because of their size). Among all the beetles, I think these are my favourite and it partly has to do with a Pokemon named Heracross which created an impression on a young me.
